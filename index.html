ARTISANAL GRINDING & ROASTING

Great South Indian filter coffee begins with the grind. Our slow-roasted Arabica and Robusta beans are milled to a customized medium-fine consistency tailored exclusively for brass filter percolation.

No Powder Leakage: Milled fine enough for rich extraction without falling through filter perforations.
Optimal Drip Rate: Allows hot water to steep for 5–7 minutes yielding a thick, syrupy decoction.
Aroma-Locked Packaging: Ground fresh before packaging into multi-layer foil to seal volatile aromatic oils.

HOW TO BREW THE PERFECT FILTER COFFEE

THE SACRED KAAPI RITUAL

Extracted directly from the TEMPLEBREW packaging artwork guidelines for authentic, frothy degree kaapi.

01

THE MEASURE

Add 1 to 2 heaped tablespoons of fresh TEMPLEBREW powder into the upper perforated cup of the brass filter.

Use freshly opened pack
02

THE TAMP

Press the coffee powder lightly using the perforated umbrella pressing disc. Do not press too hard.

Ensures uniform extraction
03

THE STEEP

Pour 50-60 ml of freshly boiled water over the disc. Close the brass lid and allow 5 to 7 minutes for thick decoction to collect.

Liquid gold decoction
04

METER FROTH

Mix hot boiled milk and sugar. Pour between brass tumbler and dabara from a height to generate thick golden foam.

Authentic South Indian pour

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about TEMPLEBREW degree coffee.

What makes TEMPLEBREW Degree Coffee special?

Our coffee is slow-roasted in artisanal batches following the heritage recipe of temple towns like Srirangam and Kumbakonam. We use 80% highland coffee beans and 20% select French chicory to create the thick, aromatic decoction that authentic filter kaapi is renowned for.

What does "Degree Coffee" mean?

In South Indian coffee lore, "Degree" originally referred to using pure, unadulterated cow's milk tested with a lactometer (degree), and brewing with first-degree decoction (the thickest, purest first percolation dripping through the brass filter).

How should I store the coffee powder?

Keep the pack tightly closed after opening, or transfer into an airtight brass, stainless steel, or glass container away from direct sunlight and moisture. This keeps the aroma fresh for up to 12 months.

Do you ship across Tamil Nadu and India?

Yes! We pack fresh roasts daily at our Bikshandarkoil roastery near Srirangam and dispatch directly via courier across India. You can order easily through WhatsApp at 80724 35415.
